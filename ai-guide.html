Read & summarise (AI) guide

Read & summarise reads a folder of documents (text PDFs, Word, Excel and emails) and turns them into the results you need: invoices entered in Excel, a brief of a court case, a calendar of due dates, an email draft, a register, or one PDF to hand in. Every value is checked against the document before it is used.

Read & summarise is part of SoloCopy Pro. The free version can try it on 10 files.

Our promise: nothing wrong written as fact

  • A value is used only when the document proves it: the AI must quote the line it read, and SoloCopy checks that the line really says that value.
  • Dates go into a calendar only under their own label on the same line, for example “Next date of hearing”, “Due date” or “Valid until”.
  • Where a document sums something up (a court's order, a notice's demand), the brief shows the document's own words in quotation marks, never the AI's paraphrase.
  • Invoices are checked by arithmetic, GSTIN check digits and a second reading of the amounts. Only invoices that pass every check go on the Verified sheet; the others go on Needs review with the reason.
  • Something that cannot be proven is left out or asked about. Missing is allowed; wrong is not. Still, check a result before you rely on it.

The five steps

  1. Choose a folder. Open Tools > Read & summarise and pick the folder of documents. Your files are only read, never changed.
  2. Read. SoloCopy reads every file and tells you what it could not read and why.
  3. Summary. Each file and the whole folder in a few lines, each line with the file and page it comes from. A line is kept only if that page supports it.
  4. Questions. If a value is unclear, you are asked about it. Your answer is used from then on.
  5. Create. Choose the kind of documents, then the result you need. You see a preview first; nothing is written until you click Create. Choose where the results are saved.

Check CVs against the job

Type the must-have and nice-to-have requirements, for example C#, SQL Server, B.E.. Each one is looked for in every CV as a whole word or phrase (“Java” does not match “JavaScript”), and the CV line that has it is shown. There is no AI score and nothing is decided for you: CVs are only sorted, and you read them and decide. SoloCopy can prepare an invitation draft or a polite “not shortlisted” draft for each candidate; drafts are never sent.

Finishing the job: moves and email drafts

  • Email drafts are only drafts. They open in Outlook; you check them and send them yourself. SoloCopy never sends email.
  • Moving files only after you confirm. After entering invoices you can move them to a Done folder; after checking CVs you can move the ones you decided on to Not shortlisted. You see the list and tick what moves. Every move is in History, and Restore puts the file back. Nothing is deleted.

Where the AI runs

Choose in Tools > AI settings:

  • Offline and Offline (light): an AI model on this PC (Ollama, installed separately). Nothing about your documents leaves the PC.
  • Hybrid: the text of your documents is sent to Google Gemini with an API key you create yourself. Email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and other long ID or account numbers are replaced before sending; names, addresses and other words are not masked. With a free Google key, Google's terms allow it to use the text to improve its services, so use Offline for documents you may not share.

Your IT team can allow only the offline modes. Read texts are kept on this PC, encrypted for your Windows account, and deleted after 30 days. See the privacy policy.

What it does not do

  • It does not read scanned pages or photos (no OCR) in this version.
  • It does not send email, delete files or decide about people.
  • It can be wrong. Values it cannot prove are left out or asked about, but check every result before you use it.
